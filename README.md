## 👋 About Me

I'm a **final-year BSBA student** at **Tunis Business School, University of Tunis**, majoring in **Information Technology** and minoring in **Business Analytics**.

My projects span computer vision, backend development, and data analytics. During my internship at **Instaware**, I benchmarked computer vision models and built a live demo to compare their predictions side by side.

- 🏆 **1st place out of 16 teams** with FertiGrow at the 2025 Youth Speak Forum Bardo Hackathon
